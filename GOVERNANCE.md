# RagZen Governance Model

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Maintainers**: Responsible for reviewing PRs, managing releases, and guiding project architecture.
- **Contributors**: Anyone who submits code, documentation, issue reports, or feedback.

## Decision Making Process

1. Major architecture changes require an Implementation Plan issue/PR.
2. Breaking changes require a Major version bump per Semantic Versioning.
3. PRs require approval from at least one Maintainer.
